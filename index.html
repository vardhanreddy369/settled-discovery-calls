15 minutes on how your events actually run

We are five UCF engineering students. For one semester our whole assignment is to talk to people who run events. Nothing to sell, we only want to learn.

Pick a time
  • 15 min 20 at the most
  • Video call link sent when you book
  • Two of us one asks, one takes notes
  • Afterwards nothing at all
The six questions we will ask

The whole list, so you can see it before agreeing to anything. Happy to skip anything that does not apply.

  1. 01Walk us through the last event you ran, from the first budget to the final numbers.
  2. 02Where do those numbers first live, and who else needs to see them?
  3. 03How do you track costs and approvals once the event is underway?
  4. 04What happens when a cost changes after the budget is set?
  5. 05With several events running at once, how do you keep them straight?
  6. 06Has a final number ever not matched what was expected? What happened?
Why a student is emailing you

EGN 4641C is UCF's engineering entrepreneurship course. The first half of the semester is nothing but customer interviews, because before anyone is allowed to build something they have to find out whether the problem is real.

So this is not a pitch dressed up as research. Talking to you is the assignment.

The team: Medha, Dee, Beenish, Julian and Vardhan. Two of us join every call.
